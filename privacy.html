Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Nonna ("we," "the service"), operated by James Plaschke, a sole proprietor, is a personal text-messaging assistant that remembers your pantry, recipe, and cooking preferences so you don't have to repeat yourself every time you ask for a meal idea.

What we collect

When you text Nonna, we store: your phone number, the content of your messages, and information you share with the bot (pantry items, food preferences, recipes, and cooking history). We also store links you share and content we fetch from them to extract recipes.

How we use it

This information is used solely to operate the service — to remember your preferences across conversations and give you relevant responses. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Third parties

Messages are processed using Anthropic's Claude API to generate responses, and delivered via Twilio's SMS network. These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy and security terms; we do not share your data with any other third party.

Data retention

Your data is retained as long as you continue using the service. You can request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at the email below.

Mobile information

We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Message frequency varies depending on your usage of the service. Message and data rates may apply.

Opting out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for assistance.
