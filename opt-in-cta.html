Nonna — Where users see the opt-in call to action

Nonna (operated by James Plaschke, a sole proprietor) is promoted through short-form video and photo slideshows on TikTok (@textnonna). The final slide of every post shows:

Text Nonna.
No app. No signup.
(571) 714-8805
Text to start

The post caption repeats the call to action together with the required disclosure:

Text Nonna at (571) 714-8805 for recipe suggestions, saved-recipe confirmations, and pantry and meal-planning replies. Msg frequency depends on your activity. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help. Terms and Privacy: https://james-plaschke.github.io/kitchen-bot-legal/start.html

Consumers can also open the public opt-in page, which includes an explicit consent checkbox before the text button is enabled: start.html

No one is messaged unless they text the number first.
